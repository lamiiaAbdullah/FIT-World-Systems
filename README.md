# 🏋️ FIT World — Fitness Tracker App

A fitness tracking application designed to help users maintain a healthy lifestyle by tracking calories, water intake, and workouts — with personalized reminders and trainer support.

## 📌 Project Overview
Built as part of the Software Engineering course (SE 2111). The goal is to empower individuals to achieve their health goals through a personalized and motivating experience.

## 🎯 Problem
- People struggle to maintain healthy habits due to lack of motivation and knowledge
- Most apps focus only on exercise and ignore nutrition and hydration
- No personalized guidance based on individual needs

## 💡 Solution
FIT World provides a comprehensive platform that includes:
- 🔥 Calorie tracking based on personal goals (weight loss / maintenance)
- 💧 Water intake reminders
- 🏃 Workout tracking with custom exercises
- 💬 Live chat with professional trainers
- 🔔 Smart reminder notifications

## ✨ Key Features
- Personalized onboarding (age, weight, height, goals)
- Daily calorie and water tracking
- Fitness goal setting and progress tracking
- Friend management and social motivation
- Trainer communication system
- Two-factor authentication for security

## 🛠️ Technologies Used
- **Design:** Figma
- **Architecture:** UML (Use Case, Sequence, Class Diagrams)
- **Type:** Mobile Application (Concept & UI Design)
